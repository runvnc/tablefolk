Graphics

      
Everything below defaults to the original look. Turning things down trades a little visual fidelity for smoother frames - worth trying if the world feels heavy or you notice audio glitching while things render.

      
Quality

      

        
      

      Medium keeps the detailed island's plastic look and normal maps with far less geometry, 1k textures and no ambient occlusion. Light is a simple voxel island with no shadows or post effects. Use medium or light if the screen goes black or the tab crashes with the larger model (Gemma 4 E4B). Changing this reloads the page.
      
While a character is thinking

      

        
      

      The model shares the GPU with the 3D view. Lower = faster replies; paused freezes the view until the reply arrives.
      
Shadows

      

        
        
        
        
      

      Each is a real-time point-light shadow (renders 6 cubemap faces every refresh). "Refresh every 2 frames" halves that cost; disabling stops the map updating entirely.
      
Sea

      

        
        
      

      Both rebuild geometry on the CPU every frame. Foam is the cheaper/less-noticeable of the two if you just want to shave a little cost.
