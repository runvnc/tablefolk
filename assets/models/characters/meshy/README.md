Monsters generated for Tablefolk with Meshy v6 text-to-3D and Meshy auto-rigging
(through fal.ai), paid generations owned by the project: `tools/fal-monster.py`
makes one, `tools/merge-clips.mjs` merges its clips (Idle, Walk, Attack,
HitReact, Death), `tools/compress-glb.sh` compresses it.

The people (`swordswoman sorceress huntress villager_woman swordsman
wanderer`, 2026-10-25) are Meshy v6 image-to-3D (PBR) from concept images made
with fal.ai (FLUX), rigged the same way: `tools/fal-character.py`.
