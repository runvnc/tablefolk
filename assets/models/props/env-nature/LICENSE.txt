Trees and plants in this folder (and cover/) were generated procedurally for
Tablefolk by tools/blender/make-trees.py and make-cover.py (branch skeletons
grown with the Sapling Tree Gen add-on for Blender; its GPL licence covers the
add-on, not the meshes it produces). The meshes are released under CC0 1.0
(public domain), like the rest of the generated assets.

Textures are made from these CC0 1.0 (public domain) assets by Poly Haven
(https://polyhaven.com, licence: https://polyhaven.com/license):

  bark
    jolcham_oak_bark_01_*.avif   Jolcham Oak Bark 01   https://polyhaven.com/a/jolcham_oak_bark_01
    pine_bark_*.avif             Pine Bark             https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_bark
    tree_bark_03_*.avif          Tree Bark 03          https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_bark_03
    bark_brown_02_*.avif         Bark Brown 02         https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02
    bark_willow_02_*.avif        Bark Willow 02        https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_willow_02

  leaf-cluster atlases (leaves_broadleaf, leaves_autumn, leaves_conifer;
  tools/blender/make-leaf-atlases.py cuts single leaves out of these models'
  textures, recolours them and arranges them into sprays)
    Island Tree 01      https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_01      (elliptic leaves: oak, autumn)
    Shrub 01            https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_01            (serrated leaf: tall_tree)
    Shrub 03            https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_03            (heart leaves: tree, autumn)
    Shrub 04            https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_04            (small leaves: bushes)
    Fir Tree 01         https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_tree_01         (needle sprays)
    Pine Tree 01        https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01        (long-needle spray)
    Fern 02             https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02             (fern fronds)
    Celandine 01        https://polyhaven.com/a/celandine_01        (flower heads: blossoms, wildflowers)

  cover/cover_atlas.avif: grass blades drawn by the script (no source image);
  flower heads from Celandine 01 above.
